# Death certificate

## Steps
1. Deaths in a hospital are reported by the hospital; ask them for the registration.
2. For deaths at home, a family member registers on crsorgi.gov.in or at the Nagar Nigam zonal
   office within 21 days.
3. Collect or download the certificate after verification.

## Documents
- Hospital death summary, or a certificate from the cremation / burial ground
- ID proof of the deceased and of the applicant
- Affidavit if registering late

## Fees and time
Free within 21 days; a late fee applies after that, and after one year an SDM / magistrate
order is needed. Usually ready in about 7 working days. A death certificate is needed for
widow pension, property transfer and insurance claims.
