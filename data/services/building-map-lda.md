# Building map approval

## Steps
1. Get the building plan drawn by a registered architect.
2. Apply through LDA's online building permission system with the ownership documents.
3. Pay the fees shown after scrutiny. Start construction only after approval.

## If you get a notice for unauthorised construction
Read the date by which you must reply. Reply in writing before that date with your approved
map (if any) and ownership papers. You can apply for compounding (regularisation) of eligible
deviations. If sealing or demolition is ordered, you can appeal within the time written on the
order. Consult a lawyer for demolition notices.
