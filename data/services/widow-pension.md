# Widow pension

## Who can apply
Widowed women aged 18 or above living in UP whose family income is below the scheme limit.

## Steps
1. Apply online on the SSPY portal (sspy-up.gov.in) or at a Jan Seva Kendra.
2. Upload the husband's death certificate and other documents.
3. After verification by the district office, the pension is paid into your bank account.

## Documents
- Husband's death certificate
- Aadhaar card, income certificate, address proof
- Bank passbook and a photo
