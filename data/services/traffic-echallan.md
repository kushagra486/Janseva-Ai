# Traffic e-challan

## Check and pay
1. Open echallan.parivahan.gov.in and search by challan number, vehicle number or DL number.
2. Check the photo and offence details, then pay online.
3. Download the receipt.

## If you think it is wrong
Do not pay; choose to contest it. Unpaid challans are sent to the virtual court, where you can
respond online.
