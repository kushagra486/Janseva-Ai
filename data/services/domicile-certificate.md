# Domicile certificate

## Steps
1. Apply on e-District UP (edistrict.up.gov.in) or at a Jan Seva Kendra.
2. Fill in how long you have lived at the address and upload proof.
3. The Lekhpal verifies and the Tehsildar approves.

## Documents
- Aadhaar card
- Address proof such as an electricity bill, property tax receipt or rent agreement
- Self-declaration and a photo

## Fees and time
A small service fee at the Jan Seva Kendra; usually 7 to 15 working days.
