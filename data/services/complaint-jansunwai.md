# Public grievance (Jansunwai)

## Steps
1. File online on jansunwai.up.nic.in or call the CM helpline 1076.
2. Choose the department and describe the problem with the location.
3. Note the complaint number and track its status online.

## For civic problems in Lucknow
Garbage, drains, roads and streetlights can also be reported to Lucknow Nagar Nigam on 1533,
or through JANSEVA's report feature, which routes it to the right department.
