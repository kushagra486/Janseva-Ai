# Property tax (gṛhakar) in Lucknow

## What the demand notice means
Lucknow Nagar Nigam sends a yearly demand notice for property (house) tax, often together with
water tax (jalkar) and sewer charges. It shows your property ID (bhavan sankhya), the financial
year, the amount due and the last date. Paying late adds a surcharge, and long-pending dues can
lead to recovery proceedings.

## How to pay online
1. Open the Lucknow Nagar Nigam portal (lmc.up.nic.in) and go to property tax payment.
2. Search with your property ID / house number from the notice.
3. Check the owner name and amount, then pay by UPI, card or net banking.
4. Download and keep the receipt.

## How to pay offline
Pay at your zonal office tax counter with the notice. Ask for a printed receipt.

## If the amount looks wrong
File an objection (aapatti) at the zonal office before the last date, with the notice, your
previous receipts and property documents. Keep a stamped copy of your objection.

## Helpline
Lucknow Nagar Nigam call centre: 1533 (check current timings).
