# Ration card

## Types
- Antyodaya (AAY) for the poorest families
- Priority Household (Paatra Grihasthi) for eligible low-income families

## Steps
1. Apply online on the Food and Civil Supplies portal (fcs.up.gov.in) or at a Jan Seva Kendra.
2. Add every family member with their Aadhaar number.
3. The supply inspector verifies eligibility.
4. After approval, collect ration from your allotted fair price shop.

## Documents
- Aadhaar of every family member
- Income certificate, address proof, bank passbook and a photo of the head of family (usually
  the eldest woman)

## Fees and time
There is no fee to apply on the portal. Verification usually takes about 30 days.
