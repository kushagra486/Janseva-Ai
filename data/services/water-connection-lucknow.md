# New water connection

## Steps
1. Apply at the Jal Kal Vibhag zonal office or through the Nagar Nigam portal.
2. Submit the form with property documents and a clear property tax receipt.
3. A Jal Kal engineer inspects the site and tells you the connection charges.
4. Pay the charges; the connection is installed after payment.

## Documents
- Ownership proof (sale deed / registry) or the owner's consent if you are a tenant
- Latest property tax (gṛhakar) receipt with no dues
- Aadhaar card and a photo

## Fees and time
Charges depend on pipe size and road-cutting. The engineer's estimate tells you the exact
amount. Usually 15 to 30 days.

## Water bill problems
For wrong bills or no supply, contact the Jal Kal zonal office or the Nagar Nigam call
centre 1533.
