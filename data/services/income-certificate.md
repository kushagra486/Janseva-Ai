# Income certificate

## Steps
1. Apply online on e-District UP (edistrict.up.gov.in) or at any Jan Seva Kendra (CSC).
2. Enter family income from all sources and upload the documents.
3. The Lekhpal verifies, and the Tehsildar approves the application.
4. Download the certificate using your application number.

## Documents
- Aadhaar card
- Self-declaration of income (form available on the portal)
- Ration card or address proof, and a passport-size photo
- Salary slip or other income proof if you have one

## Fees and time
A small service fee is charged at a Jan Seva Kendra (check the current rate at the
centre). Usually issued in 7 to 15 working days. Needed for scholarships, pensions and many
welfare schemes.
