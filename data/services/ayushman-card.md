# Ayushman card

## Check if you are eligible
Check with your Aadhaar or ration card on beneficiary.nha.gov.in or at a Jan Seva Kendra /
empanelled hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk.

## Make the card
1. Log in with mobile OTP, verify with Aadhaar (OTP or fingerprint).
2. Download the card. Show it at any empanelled hospital for cashless treatment.

## Fees
Making the card is free.
