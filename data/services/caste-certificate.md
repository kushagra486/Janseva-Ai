# Caste certificate

## Steps
1. Apply on e-District UP (edistrict.up.gov.in) or at a Jan Seva Kendra.
2. Choose SC, ST or OBC and fill in family details.
3. The Lekhpal and Revenue Inspector verify, then the Tehsildar approves.
4. Download the certificate with your application number.

## Documents
- Aadhaar card and a photo
- A family member's caste certificate, if available
- Self-declaration and address proof

## Fees and time
A small service fee at the Jan Seva Kendra. Usually 7 to 15 working days, longer if field
verification is needed.
