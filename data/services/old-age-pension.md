# Old age pension

## Who can apply
Residents of UP aged 60 or above whose family income is below the limit set by the
department (see the scheme finder for the current limit).

## Steps
1. Apply online on the SSPY portal (sspy-up.gov.in) or at a Jan Seva Kendra.
2. Upload the documents and submit. Note the registration number.
3. After verification, the pension is paid into your bank account every quarter.

## Documents
- Aadhaar card, age proof
- Income certificate
- Bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar) and a photo
