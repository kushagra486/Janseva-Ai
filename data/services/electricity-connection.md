# Electricity connection and bills

## New connection
1. Apply online through the UPPCL consumer portal (Jhatpat connection) or at the sub-division
   office.
2. Upload ID proof, ownership or tenancy proof, and a photo.
3. Pay the estimate shown after the site survey. The meter is installed after payment.

## Paying a bill
Pay on the UPPCL website or app, at a Jan Seva Kendra, or at the sub-division office.

## Wrong bill or no power
Call the UPPCL helpline 1912. For a wrong reading, complain before paying and keep the
complaint number.
