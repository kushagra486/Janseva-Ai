# Marriage registration

## Steps
1. Apply online on the IGRS UP portal (igrsup.gov.in) under marriage registration.
2. Fill in details of both spouses and two witnesses and upload the documents.
3. Book a date and visit the sub-registrar office with both spouses and witnesses.
4. The certificate is issued after verification.

## Documents
- Age proof and Aadhaar of both spouses
- Marriage invitation card or photo of the marriage
- ID of two witnesses, and address proof

## Fees
A registration fee is paid online; check the current fee on the portal.
