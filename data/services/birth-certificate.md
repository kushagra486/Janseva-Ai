# Birth certificate

## Who registers
Births in a hospital are usually reported by the hospital. For home births, a parent or
family member registers the birth. Registration within 21 days of birth is free under the
Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969.

## Steps
1. If the baby was born in a hospital, ask the hospital whether it has registered the birth.
2. Otherwise register online on the Civil Registration System portal (crsorgi.gov.in) or at
   the Nagar Nigam zonal office birth and death registration counter.
3. Fill in the child's details, parents' names and address, and upload the documents.
4. After verification, download the certificate from the portal or collect it from the office.

## Documents
- Hospital discharge slip or birth report (for hospital births)
- Parents' ID proof (Aadhaar or voter ID) and address proof
- An affidavit for home births or late registration

## Fees and time
Free if registered within 21 days. A small late fee applies after 21 days. After one year the
birth can only be registered with an order from the SDM / magistrate. The certificate is
usually ready within 7 working days of verification. Check the current late fee at the office.
