# Aadhaar address update

## Online
1. Log in on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in with your Aadhaar number and mobile OTP.
2. Choose address update and upload a valid address proof (rent agreement, electricity bill,
   bank passbook).
3. Note the URN and track the status online.

## At an Aadhaar centre
Visit an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or enrolment centre with the original address proof. A fee
applies at the centre (₹50 at the time of writing; check the current fee).

## Time
Usually updated within a few days to 30 days.
