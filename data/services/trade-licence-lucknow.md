# Trade licence

## Steps
1. Apply at the Nagar Nigam licence section or through the Nagar Nigam portal.
2. Give the business type, address and owner details.
3. Pay the licence fee, which depends on the trade and area.
4. Renew it every year before it expires.

## Documents
- Aadhaar and photo of the owner
- Proof of the shop premises (rent agreement or ownership) and a property tax receipt

## Street vendors
Street vendors are registered by the Town Vending Committee under the Street Vendors Act,
2014. Ask your zonal office about vending certificates. See also the PM SVANidhi loan scheme.
